# Original game artwork

The vector assets in this directory were created specifically for Spaghetti Busters. They have no external dependencies, fonts, linked images, or third-party artwork.

- `character.svg`: 280 × 430 view box. White-suited code quality specialist with glasses, yellow tie, swept dark hair, and a quality-check lapel badge. Faces right.
- `cannon.svg`: 255 × 135 view box. Separate mint-and-yellow refactoring cannon, faces right. The suggested rotation pivot is (32, 67), the approximate muzzle center is (237, 66).
- `city.svg`: 1280 × 720 view box. Layered mint cartoon server city. Ground begins at y=611; central sky is intentionally open for gameplay.
- `hero-art.svg`: 1280 × 900 view box. Transparent foreground illustration for the opening scene. Giant specialist occupies the right side, firing left, with floating TypeScript cards and comic effects.
